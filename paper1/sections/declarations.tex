\section*{Statements and Declarations}

\subsection*{Data availability}
Experimental manifests, fixed sample identifiers, predictions and scoring
scripts are available at
\url{https://github.com/turambar928/MCP_based_KG_construction}.
The experiment READMEs identify the original datasets, their access points
and the transformations used in this study. Anonymized aggregate human-review
results and the frozen sampling protocol are provided with the experiment;
individual responses, free-text reasons, and source context are retained
privately.

\subsection*{Code availability}
The same repository contains the implementation, rendered prompts,
configuration manifests and commands for reproducing the reported analyses.
